# Breeze Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Breeze is a Mac utility that runs entirely on your computer. We built it so that your files never leave your Mac.

## What Breeze does not collect

- No account, no sign-up.
- No analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising identifiers.
- No file names, file contents, app lists, browsing data or scan results are ever sent anywhere.

## The only network requests Breeze makes

1. **Update checks.** About once a day (you can turn this off in Settings), Breeze downloads a small file describing the latest version from our website. No personal information is included.
2. **App update checks (only when you open the Updater).** Breeze asks each app's own update server whether a newer version exists, exactly as that app would itself.

## Data stored on your Mac

- Settings, in Breeze's preferences.
- A history of items Breeze removed, in `~/Library/Application Support/Breeze/history.jsonl` and `~/Library/Logs/Breeze/breeze.log`. You can delete these at any time.

## Crash reports

If Breeze crashes, it offers to open an email to us with the crash report attached. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.

## Tips

Breeze is free. If you choose to leave a tip, Gumroad handles the payment as the merchant of record, and we receive your email address and order details from them. Breeze itself never contacts Gumroad.

## Source code

Breeze is open source, so you can check every claim above yourself: https://github.com/nanket/breeze
